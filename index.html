    03 · Westside LOV · brand campaign · spec · 16:9

    A 15-second brand film from 18 stills, every cut written into one prompt.

    Six outfits, three angles each, shuffled into 18 beats so no outfit plays twice in a row, then written into one timecoded Seedance 2.5 prompt. Two drafts pushed the camera harder; a frame-by-frame check kept a stray drone out of the final cut.

    04 · ComfyUI · women's catalogue images · personal tool · 3:4

    One flat lay in, five catalogue shots out, all on one studio backdrop.

    A 192-node ComfyUI graph on Comfy Cloud. Pick an AI actor, drop in a flat lay, press Run: GPT Image 2.5 renders five 3:4 shots, each posed from a library photo. DWPose passes only the skeleton, so the reference model's face and clothes never leak in. A grey-card grade (SAM3 masks and a GLSL shader) then matches every backdrop to one studio plate without cutting anyone out.

    5Shots per run
